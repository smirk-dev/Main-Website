About

Suryansh Mishra

AI engineer. Co-founder and CTO of Alethman.

I'm Suryansh Mishra, an AI engineer from Noida, India. I'm the co-founder and CTO of Alethman, where I built the engine that follows how news stories form and spread and sets them beside the record. I'm also a forward deployed engineer at InboxKit.

Most of my work is LLM agents, retrieval-augmented generation pipelines and the back-end and cloud systems under them. I study computer science at UPES with a minor in AI from IIT Ropar, I contribute to Rocket.Chat, and I've won three national hackathons. Outside of code I paint, write poetry and play football.
