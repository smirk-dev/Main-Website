Gate 1 · Structures: candidates for your approval

These are candidates for the 7 category structures that stand along the paths of the Act III pine forest. Each one is a found object, not a sign, and each is the key to one content category. Everything here is free: CC0 (no credit needed) or CC Attribution (credited on the site). Nothing is non-commercial, no-derivatives or editorial-only. Real scans are preferred over modelled props, and each card says which it is. Click any image to rotate the model in 3D. Gold border = my recommendation. Pick one per category, or tell me which categories you'd like me to search again.

Face counts are for the full-detail originals. Anything over ~300k faces will be simplified for the website, as with the forest. Poly Haven thumbnails are shown on black and Sketchfab ones in each author's own lighting, so compare the objects, not the backgrounds.
