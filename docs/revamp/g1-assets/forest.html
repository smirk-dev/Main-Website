Gate 1 · Forest and door: candidates for your approval

Everything here is CC0 from Poly Haven: free, no credit required, and photoscanned. Most of it is from Poly Haven's matched pine forest collection, so the lighting and colour agree across the whole set, which is what makes a forest read as real instead of collaged. Gold border = my recommendation. Approve or reject per card. The 13 non-hero statues will also live in this forest.

Poly counts are the full-detail originals. The website uses the lower-detail versions Poly Haven ships, plus our own simplified copies and flat stand-ins for distant trees, so the forest stays fast on ordinary machines.
Gap: Poly Haven has few big broadleaf trees (oak, beech). If you want a mixed forest rather than a mostly pine one, I'll look for free oak/beech scans on Sketchfab once your token is in.
