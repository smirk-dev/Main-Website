Gate 2 · Forest v09: all 28 structures, in 7 clusters

The main view (unchanged composition). The ruined arch and the milestones are now faint shapes in the mist beyond the fork angel. As with the statues, structures are dim from a distance and only reveal themselves up close.

The seven clusters, up close

Each category is one cluster: my recommended piece in the middle, the other three around it.

Landing: lanterns. First thing off the trail. Hurricane lanterns hang from three old posts and sit on the floor, with the five brass lamps on the tallest arm. Each one is lit; the glow is faint in daylight and will read far more on the site at dusk.

Earlier: wells and mirrors. Left of the trail before the fork: the Venice well, the cheval mirror and the gilded mirror, with the Dinan well behind.

Stories: the ruined arch. It spans the main trail, so you walk through it, with the stone gate, garden folly and fortress arch around it.

Games: the stone ring. Past the Grim Reaper at the end of the left branch: a Callanish-style ring in a clearing. The chess set is left mid-game on the concrete table, with the mossy stone table beside it. (This camera has a trunk in frame; I'll find a cleaner angle.)

Work: the milestones. Past the Black Angel at the end of the right branch. The Richmond stone's carved "VIII MILES" reads close up. (This angle is a little far; an earlier one was better and I'll restore it.)

Art: easels and a writing desk. The crank easel in the middle, the paint-dripped easel beside it, and the typewriter on the desk.

Tools: anvils and instruments. The anvil on its mossy stump, the old anvil, the armillary sphere and the astrolabe.
